#About Me
My name is Bryan McCanch. I maintain a strict standard of academic excellence in mechanical engineering, aiming to stand out through rigorous mathematical modeling grounded in core physical principles. Beyond the classroom, I serve as an active member of the Formula SAE (FSAE) team, where my primary function is developing physical components for a student-built racecar. This work requires applying computer-aided design (CAD) alongside stress analysis to solve complex problems under strict spatial and weight constraints.

Every design choice I make on the FSAE racecar is guided by data rather than intuition. Decisions regarding geometry and material selection are backed by simulation data paired with physical testing, explicitly justifying why each part was implemented in a specific way. Grounded in solid mechanics and material science, my work focuses on turning theoretical equations into functional hardware, ensuring that every design satisfies required safety factors and structural loads without adding unnecessary mass.

I organized this portfolio to provide clear, technical documentation of my engineering work. The content within gives the viewer direct insight into my academic foundation alongside my ability to deliver verified engineering solutions. In total, this took me close to 6 hours to do in an effective way.

To defend an engineering decision is to back it up by empirical data that is relevant, accurate, and non-misleading.
